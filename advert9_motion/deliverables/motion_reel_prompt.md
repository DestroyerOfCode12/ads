# Motion reel prompt, repurposed for Nimble Tech Solutions

Source idea: "make a dynamic 15-second motion graphics video that shows what an incredible motion
designer you are for <website url>" (@melvynx, via prompt-motion.com). Rewritten for this business:

---

Make a dynamic 15 second vertical motion graphics advert (1080 by 1920, 30 fps, exactly 450 frames,
MP4, plus a silent copy with no audio stream) that shows off top tier motion design for Nimble Tech
Solutions, an owner run web and IT business in Midrand serving small businesses in greater
Johannesburg and remote clients. Style: product UI plus kinetic typography, like a premium SaaS promo
reel, but in this brand: teal 115E67, warm white F7F5F0, mist D9ECEC, deep ink 18282A, apricot F2B66D;
DejaVu Sans and DejaVu Sans Bold; the approved logo (serif "Nimble" in P052 over sans "Tech Solutions")
untouched; the light angular pattern as a subtle moving background only.

Beats (120 bpm, one beat = 15 frames, cut on the beat):
1. 0 to 1.5 s: "Your / business, / online" slams up through masks with springy overshoot and an
   apricot underline swipe; the "o" of online bursts into a teal circle wipe.
2. 1.5 to 4.5 s: a browser frame draws itself, then the advert 1 website assembles (nav strip, hero with
   "Your business, online" typing in letter by letter, two content blocks popping on springs), swings in
   3D and flies out.
3. 4.5 to 9.5 s: a masked vertical ticker cycles the five services, each with a UI vignette: Websites
   (three pages fan out), Business email (message cards slide in and stack), Hosting (servers rise, LEDs
   blink, a pulse line draws, a lock pops), SEO (a search types "web design Midrand", a pin drops onto a
   map with a ripple), Video adverts (play morphs to pause, scrubber runs, film strip slides).
4. 9.5 to 12.8 s: a tilted marquee grid of all those UI tiles, columns running in opposite directions,
   the service words punching in on the beat; the camera dives into the centre tile.
5. 12.8 to 15 s: teal end card: logo rises, then Websites and digital services, WhatsApp 068 178 9708,
   Midrand, Johannesburg, Remote; fully still from 13.5 s.

Craft: springs and eased curves, never linear; staggered reveals; real motion blur (average several sub
frame samples per frame). Original synthesised score and sound design that hits every beat; effects stay
under the music; two pass linear loudness normalisation to about minus 14 LUFS, true peak checked on the
finished MP4. UK English, no hyphens or dashes, no prices, client names, results, ratings or claims.
Keep settled text inside x 96 to 904, y 154 to 1536, verified per frame. Strip metadata and tool names.
