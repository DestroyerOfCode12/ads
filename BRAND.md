# Nimble Tech Solutions: advert brief

The standing rules for every advert in this repository. Read this before building one.

## Name and contact
- Visible brand name: **Nimble Tech Solutions**, exactly. Never "Nimble IT", never a legal suffix in adverts.
- Contact line: WhatsApp 068 178 9708
- Area line: Midrand, Johannesburg, Remote
- BBBEE: application pending. Never imply certification.

## Services shown in adverts
Website design and development, business email setup and management, web hosting,
search engine optimisation (SEO), video advertising. (CV work and small IT tasks stay out of adverts.)

Audience: small business owners in greater Johannesburg, Midrand base, plus remote clients.

## Brand identity
- Colours: digital teal 115E67, warm white F7F5F0, mist D9ECEC, deep ink 18282A, apricot F2B66D
- Logo: outlined serif wordmark, "Nimble" in P052 serif, "Tech Solutions" in a compact sans, plus an "N"
  symbol; primary, horizontal and stacked versions, teal and white, supplied as SVG in the branding pack
  (Nimble_Tech_Solutions_Branding_Pack). Keep the logo intact; never redraw it.
  **The approved SVGs are not in this repository yet.** Adverts 7 and 8 use a typographic stand in;
  put the white stacked SVG (or a transparent PNG export) at `advert7v2/assets/logo_white.png` to replace it.
- Type outside the logo: DejaVu Sans and DejaVu Sans Bold
- A light angular pattern is approved as a decorative background, never as a logo substitute
- Motion: restrained position and opacity transitions, a still end state

## Copy rules
- UK English; plain, capable, approachable, practical
- No hyphens, en dashes or em dashes in visible copy
- No prices, client names, client results, testimonials, ratings or unconfirmed credentials
  (exception: a pricing advert the owner asks for; see Current prices)
- No AI or tool traces in the files (strip metadata, scan for tool names)
