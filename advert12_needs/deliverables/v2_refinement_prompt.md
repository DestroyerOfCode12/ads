# Refinement prompt: "What a website actually needs" v2

Make the advert smoother and give it one harmonious colour scheme. Keep the Nimble Tech Solutions logo
and the teal end card exactly as they are.

Colour: build the whole piece in one teal family, analogous to the brand teal 115E67, so the end card
wipe feels like the same world. Deep teal radial background (14424A at the centre to 091B1E at the edges).
Cards become translucent mist glass (mist at 6 percent, a 14 percent mist border, soft shadow); every icon
tile is brand teal with mist line icons. Headings warm white F7F5F0, supporting text mist D9ECEC at
75 percent. Apricot F2B66D is the single complementary accent, used only where it means something:
"a website.", the card numbers, the live data stream, the card highlight as the stream passes, the enquiry
bubble and "all of it.". Particles two tone: 65 percent soft mist, 35 percent apricot, no pure white.

Motion: replace springy overshoots with smooth quintic ease outs and smootherstep curves; lengthen every
entrance and exit by about half; crossfade every scene change over 12 to 16 frames with a light blur and
a slight scale on the way out, so nothing cuts or pops. Overlap the words dissolving with the particles
appearing from those same glyphs. Slow the particle stream so it reads as a flow. Card highlights breathe
on a sine curve; stepped blinking becomes smooth pulses. Motion blur raised to 6 samples per frame.

Keep the same story, copy, timing beats, score and safe margins.
